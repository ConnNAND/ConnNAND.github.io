About the Project

WinWoes is a side-hustle simulator where your goal is to make as much money as you can while avoiding having your PC restart. It's a low-stakes game that doesn't take itself too seriously, just dig around and see what it has to offer, you won't be disappointed.

Accomplishments & Lessons Learned

WinWoes is almost entirely UI, which made adding content incredibly trivial. There was no need for that much art because Godot is able to do most of what we needed for us, and I believe it managed to sell itself on the vibe we wanted perfectly.

Because the ideas were simple to implement, we actually managed to implement everything we initially sought out to, however we all ended up falling in love with the idea, which is leading us to pursue an upgraded version with more content that we can put out on Steam for a buck or two, coming soon!
