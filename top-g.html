Top G

Engine Used: Godot 4

Time Frame: July 30th 2025 - August 3rd 2025

Link: Itch.io

About the Project

Born from the annual GMTK Game Jam with the theme of "loop", Top G is a platform racer where your only opponent is yourself. Take the role of one of four different playable characters, each with their own unique ability, with as many split-screen friends as you can get together in order to sprint through the city to get the best lap time you can.

Engage with the track in different ways as your top speed rises through collecting power-ups as you run by, allowing you to take new shortcuts while avoiding obstacles. Hold on to that speed until you eventually lose control and have no time to spare. Mastery over the game's mechanics can lead you towards flying at ludicrous speeds, obtaining lap times others can only ever dream of.

Accomplishments & Lessons Learned

This project was made in collaboration with a longtime friend of mine, as I helped him through his first game jam experience. I believe that the small team and simple concept gave us plenty of time to focus on polish in ways I've rarely been able to in the past, leading to this becoming one of my personal favorite jam games I've worked on.

With the project being relatively small, and the team small as well, there were never times where we were unsure of what to work on next. We were constantly bouncing ideas back and forth while we worked collaboratively in Discord, always within arms reach when we had a question. This allowed us to always stay productive as we could work together on any issues that would crop up, rather than working independantly and spending extra time trying to solve problems alone.

I still come back to this game regularly just to run a few laps every once in a while, the gameplay is genuinely compelling and I often consider revisiting the project in order to add new ideas like a daily procedural track, however for the time being I have been far too busy, but some day it might just happen.
