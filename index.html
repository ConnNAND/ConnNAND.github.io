About Me

After graduating from Shawnee State University receiving a bachelor's degree in Digital Simulation & Game Programming, I began working at JollyWare, a studio I Co-Founded with fellow Shawnee alumni. This formal education developed my skills and introduced me to many topics, tools, and languages including Python, C, C++, C#, HTML & CSS, Javascript, GLSL, Assembly, git, Perforce, Godot & GDScript, Unreal Engine 4 & 5, Unity, Blender, Maya, and the Adobe Suite, as well as audio engineering tools like Audacity, Reaper, Ableton, and experience recording with professional equipment.

I was also introduced to scrum and gained experience working in an agile environment, complimented with project management tools like Trello and ClickUp. This experience has proved invaluable in preparing me for my work at JollyWare developing Gnomad, and leveraging this experience has lead us to many opportunities to speak with mentors through the Shawnee XP Games Accelerator program post-graduation.

Through Shawnee XP, I've gained experience building budgets, timelines, pitch decks, social media content, and other marketing materials, as well as navigating the legal side of starting a game studio. Additionally, the connections made through the accelerator allowed us to pitch to various big name investors and recieve feedback on our projects, as well as keeping in touch with several mentors for more consistent communication.

It has also allowed us many opportunities to network and table at events like SGC and GDEX, as well as connecting us with events like GDC, where we were able to present our game at the Pocket Gamer Connect's Big Indie Pitch Competition, and with this opportunity we managed to be awarded first place.
